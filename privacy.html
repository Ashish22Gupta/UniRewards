Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

At UniRewards, your privacy is important. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how you can control it.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account data: name, email, phone number, password (hashed).
  • KYC data: Aadhar/PAN document images (encrypted at rest).
  • Usage data: pages visited, challenges completed, device/browser info.
  • Payment data: UPI ID or bank details (only at redemption time).

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To verify your identity for KYC compliance.
  • To process rewards and prevent fraud.
  • To send transactional emails (OTP, reward confirmations).
  • To improve the platform through anonymized analytics.

3. Sharing Your Data

We do not sell your data. We only share it with:

  • Payment processors (Razorpay/UPI gateways) to send cashback.
  • Courier partners for physical reward delivery.
  • Regulatory authorities when legally required.

4. Data Security

All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256). KYC documents are stored in isolated, access-controlled storage and are never shared with marketing partners.

5. Cookies

We use essential cookies for session management and analytics cookies (anonymized) to understand usage patterns. You can disable non-essential cookies in your browser settings.

6. Your Rights

  • Access: request a copy of all data we hold about you.
  • Correction: update inaccurate information via your Profile page.
  • Deletion: request full account deletion (data removed within 30 days).
  • Portability: export your data in JSON format on request.

7. Data Retention

Account data is retained while your account is active. KYC documents are retained for 5 years after account closure, as required by Indian financial regulations. Analytics data is anonymized after 90 days.

8. Children's Privacy

UniRewards is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If we discover such data, we will delete it immediately.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated via email or prominent notice on the platform.

10. Contact

For any privacy concerns, email our Data Protection Officer at privacy@unirewards.com.
