rocket_launch Getting Started

How do I create an account? expand_more

Click "Get Started" on the homepage and complete the 4-step registration. You'll verify your phone via OTP and set your challenge interests.

What do I need for KYC? expand_more

Aadhar card (front & back) and PAN card. Upload JPG, PNG, or PDF files under 5MB from your Profile page.

star Earning Points

Why didn't I get points for a survey? expand_more

Points are credited only for completed surveys (not partial). If you completed a survey and didn't receive points, contact support with the survey ID.

How does the referral program work? expand_more

Share your referral code from the Dashboard. You earn 100 points when each friend completes KYC (not just signs up).

account_balance_wallet Redemption

When will I receive my cashback? expand_more

Cashback is processed within 7 business days to your registered UPI ID. You'll receive an SMS + email confirmation once sent.

My reward shows "KYC Required" — what now? expand_more

Complete KYC from your Profile page. Once verified (24–48 hrs), the "KYC Required" badge disappears and the reward becomes claimable.

error Troubleshooting

I can't log in expand_more

Try resetting your password via "Forgot Password". If the problem persists, ensure you're using the same email you registered with.

KYC upload fails expand_more

Ensure files are JPG, PNG, or PDF and under 5MB. If the issue persists, try compressing the image or contact support.
